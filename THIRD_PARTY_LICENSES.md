## Visual effects

Installed (check scenes in `game/scenes/tests/vfx_check_*.tscn`). **Wired into gameplay** (playtest ruling 5, `game/scenes/impact_looks.gd`): from Binbun Hit FX, `hit_01`, `hit_02`, `impact_01` and `big_impact_01` (the player's hit sparks, their omni light and audio player stripped) with the pack's `VFXImpactBB` script and the shared `VFXControllerBB`. The volcano ring arena (zone SOP 6c, `game/scenes/ring_fx.gd`) uses knowercoder's `3d_fire` scene as its vents' eruption column, with the pack's `ToonFire.gdshader` copied and recoloured to lava in `game/scenes/vent_fire.gdshader` (MIT: the credit line below, and the header of that file, name the source). The boons' in-combat VFX (boon-system tickets 15 and 16, `game/scenes/boon_event_vfx.gd`) use Binbun Explosion FX's `vfx_ground_explosion_01` (Fire bomb's blast; it was tried for the vents and dropped there: it does not shrink with its node's scale) and Binbun Battle FX's `vfx_blank_shield_01` (Mirror shield's reflect, recoloured silver), both stripped of light and audio like the hit sparks, and knowercoder's `3d_fire` on `vent_fire.gdshader` (Ignite's burn). The Kenney textures used in gameplay are listed under Textures.
